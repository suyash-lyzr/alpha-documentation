---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Go from a prompt to a running app in a few minutes."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Alpha">
    Go to [alpha.new](https://alpha.new) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what you want to build">
    Type your idea into the composer and send it. Your first message creates the session and starts its machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the agent work">
    The agent's commands, plans and file edits stream into the chat. Once the app is running, it appears in the **Preview**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Iterate">
    Send follow-up messages to change what you see. A message sent while the agent is working steers the current task.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Ask the agent to deploy the app. Everything you publish is listed on the **Published** page with its live address.
  </Step>
</Steps>
