Pointing attaches the exact thing you mean to your next message: an element in your running app, a marked-up picture of the page, or lines from a file. For the Preview's other controls, see [Preview your app](/workspace/preview). To upload a file of your own, use [the composer](/chat/composer#attach-files).

{/* screenshot to capture: /images/workspace/select-loop.mp4 — One click on the page puts that exact element into your next message. */}

## Choose how to point

Each way to point stages something in the composer. Nothing reaches the agent until you send.

| Way | Where it works | What the agent gets | Shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Select** (dashed pointer) | The Preview, HTML files, decks and PDFs. In Office files, BPMN diagrams and Mermaid diagrams, a plain click picks. | A chip describing the element: where it is in the code, what it says and how it is styled | None. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels. |
| **Draw** (pencil) | The Preview, HTML files, decks, PDFs, Office files, Excalidraw drawings, BPMN and Mermaid diagrams | A picture of the view with your marks, attached like an uploaded image | <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>Z</kbd> undoes a mark |
| **Add to prompt** | Selected text in the code, Markdown, PDF and Office viewers; selected cells in spreadsheets; a clicked BPMN or Mermaid element; selected shapes in Excalidraw | A chip holding the exact text or cells, with the file and lines, page or range | <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>L</kbd> |
| **Edit** (T) | The Preview, HTML files, decks | The text you retyped on the page, one change per piece of text, for the agent to put in the source | None. <kbd>Esc</kbd> twice leaves. |

Use Ctrl instead of ⌘ on Windows and Linux. **Select**, **Draw** and **Edit** switch each other off, so only one is active over a page at a time.

<Note>
Only the session's owner sees these tools. People you [share the session](/sharing) with can look at the Preview and the files, but they have no composer, so there is nothing to attach to.
</Note>

## Pick an element in the Preview

Use **Select** when words like "the second button in the card" would be ambiguous. The agent receives the element itself, not your description of it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Arm the picker">
    In the [Preview](/workspace/preview) toolbar, click **Select**. Its tooltip changes to "Click an element in the preview — Esc cancels". Until the app's page has loaded, the button is greyed out with "Element picker loads with the app's page".

    <Frame caption="The outline and label show exactly what a click will pick.">
      <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/suyash-lyzr/alpha-documentation@0bce4e6aa460e3426daece54f29c495f4565999a/images/workspace/preview-select-hover.webp" alt="The Harbor bookings session with Select on in the Preview toolbar. The pointer rests on the headline Good days start on the water., which is outlined and shaded, with a small dark label under it reading h1, the quoted headline and its size, 371x96." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the element">
    A ring and a label follow the pointer from element to element. Click the one you mean. It becomes a chip in the composer, and the picker switches off. <kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves without picking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what to change, and send">
    Type the change, such as "Make this text larger on mobile", and send. For more elements, click **Select** again for each. Picking the same element twice adds nothing.

    <Frame caption="The picked headline waits as a chip above your text.">
      <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/suyash-lyzr/alpha-documentation@0bce4e6aa460e3426daece54f29c495f4565999a/images/workspace/preview-select-chip.webp" alt="The composer of the Harbor bookings session with one chip above the typed text Make this headline smaller on phones. The chip has a dashed-pointer icon, reads h1 and the quoted start of the headline, Good days start on the wa…, and has a small X on its right. The bottom row shows the + button, GPT-6.1-Sol, xhigh, Build and a lit send button." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

The chip describes the element: a unique CSS selector, the page, its size and position, its text, the styles that differ from the defaults, nearby text and its HTML. In a React app it also names the components around the element, and in a development build the source file and line that rendered it. IDs, class names and source paths that look like secrets, such as anything containing `secret`, `password` or `api_key`, are left out or become `[redacted]` before they leave the page. The element's text and HTML go as they are.

### Attach the Preview's logs

Open **Logs** in the Preview toolbar and click the paperclip, "Attach the last lines to your next message". The last 50 lines of the open tab become a chip: **Preview console**, or the server's name followed by **log**. [Read the logs](/workspace/preview#read-the-logs) covers the panel.

## Draw on the Preview

Use **Draw** when the problem is visual, or when you want several fixes at once. Click **Draw** in the Preview toolbar and a dock appears along the bottom of the page.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Draw** | Freehand pen |
| **Arrow** | An arrow from where you press to where you let go |
| **Box** | A rectangle around an area |
| **Numbered pin** | A numbered marker, so your message can say "pin 2 should be bigger" |
| Undo (**Undo · ⌘Z**) | Removes the last mark |
| Trash (**Clear all marks**) | Removes every mark and keeps you drawing |
| X (**Cancel · Esc**) | Leaves drawing and drops the marks |
| **Add to chat** | Captures the whole page with your marks and attaches it to your next message |

The page keeps scrolling while you draw, and each mark stays pinned to what it was drawn over. **Add to chat** captures the whole page, not only the visible part, so you can mark the header and the footer in one go. The picture lands in the composer as an image attachment, and the agent is told your marks point at what you mean.

<Frame caption="Draw on the page, then send it with Add to chat.">
  <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/suyash-lyzr/alpha-documentation@0bce4e6aa460e3426daece54f29c495f4565999a/images/workspace/preview-draw-dock.webp" alt="The Harbor bookings session with Draw on in the Preview toolbar. A red line runs across the sailboat photo of the Harbor app. A dock along the bottom of the Preview holds the drawing tools, Draw, Arrow, Box and Numbered pin, then Undo, Clear all marks, Cancel and Add to chat." />

Capture gives up after 30 seconds with "The preview screenshot timed out." Marks live in the page, so a reload, such as the one at the end of each turn, clears them. Pictures up to [{imageMax}](/reference/limits) reach the model as images.

## Retype text on the page

Use **Edit** when the wording is wrong: a typo, a heading, a button label. You type the new text straight onto the page, and the agent puts it in the source.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Edit">
    In the [Preview](/workspace/preview) toolbar, click **Edit**, the T beside **Draw** and **Select**. Its tooltip changes to "Click any text on the page and type — Esc discards the changes". Until the app's page has loaded, the button is greyed out with "Editing loads with the app's page".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the text and type">
    Hovering outlines a piece of text and turns the pointer into a text cursor. Click, and the caret lands where you clicked. Type over the text as you would in any text box. <kbd>Enter</kbd>, <kbd>Esc</kbd>, <kbd>Tab</kbd> or a click elsewhere ends the typing. Change as many pieces of text as you like.

    <Frame caption="Type over any text on the page, then send the changes to the agent.">
  <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/suyash-lyzr/alpha-documentation@0bce4e6aa460e3426daece54f29c495f4565999a/images/workspace/preview-edit-text.webp" alt="The Harbor bookings session with Edit highlighted in the Preview toolbar. The app's headline has been retyped to read Good days start on the water. Every week., with a dashed outline around it while it's edited. A bar along the bottom of the Preview reads 1 text change, Clear and Send. The composer still holds a chip for the h1 headline from an earlier pick." />
</Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the changes">
    A bar along the bottom of the page counts your changes: "N text changes · **Clear** · **Send**". **Send** sends every change as one message, which starts a turn, or [steers or queues](/chat/queue-and-steer) if the agent is busy. The transcript shows one chip per change. **Clear** puts the text back as it was.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until the agent's turn ends, what you typed lives only in the page. The source changes when the agent applies your message, and the page reloads with it at the end of the turn. In the Preview, that end-of-turn reload waits while unsent changes are on the page.

| While Edit is on | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| You click a link or a button | Nothing. Links don't navigate and buttons don't fire, so you can click into their text. |
| You press <kbd>Esc</kbd> twice, click **Edit** again, or turn on **Draw** or **Select** | Edit turns off and unsent changes are dropped. The first <kbd>Esc</kbd> only ends the typing. |
| The page reloads or navigates | Unsent changes go with it. |
| The Preview shows the last version of a sleeping app | **Edit** waits, with "Editing waits for the live app, a moment away". |

Edit changes plain text only: no line breaks, bold, links or other styling, and nothing moves. Text that isn't part of the page's own text, such as what's typed in a form field, an image's alt text or text inside an SVG, can't be retyped. To change colours, sizes or spacing, point at the element with **Select** and say what you want in your message, such as "Make this button the same blue as the header".

## Add a selection from a file

Select text in a file and **Add to prompt** floats under the selection. Click it, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>L</kbd>. In the code editor it is also in the right-click menu as **Add selection to prompt**.

| Viewer | What the chip holds |
| --- | --- |
| Code | The selected lines with the file path and line range. The chip reads like `server.js:6-13`. |
| Markdown | The selected text, with the file path and the source lines it came from |
| PDF | The selected text, with the file and page |
| Word and PowerPoint | The selected text, quoted with its page or slide numbers |
| Excel, CSV and TSV | Cells, a range, a row, a column or the whole sheet, as a Markdown table plus the formulas behind it |
| Excalidraw | Each selected shape: what it is and says, an arrow's ends and its frame |

Excalidraw has no shortcut, because <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>L</kbd> is the editor's own lock key there. Use the floating button.

## Pick and draw in documents

Each viewer keeps its pointing tools at the right end of the file's header: a pencil to draw and, in PDFs, HTML files and decks, a dashed pointer to pick. In Office files, BPMN diagrams and Mermaid diagrams, a plain click picks. [File viewers](/workspace/file-viewers) covers what each viewer shows.

| File | Pick | Draw and **Add to chat** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PDF | Click a paragraph, image, chart, table or link. Hold <kbd>⇧</kbd> to narrow to one line, or <kbd>⌥</kbd> to widen to the region around it, such as the whole table around a cell. | Marks stay on their page through zoom and reload. The marked pages go as one picture labelled by page. |
| Word, Excel, PowerPoint | Click a chart, picture or shape. Word paragraphs and tables and Excel cells can't be clicked, so select them instead. | The page holds still while the pen is on |
| HTML file | Click an element, as in the Preview. **Edit** retypes text here too. | The page with your marks |
| Deck | Click an element on the slide. **Edit** retypes text here too. | The slides with your marks. See [Slides, drawings and diagrams](/workspace/decks-and-drawings). |
| BPMN diagram | Click a task, gateway or event, then **Add to prompt**. The chip holds its kind, name, lane, flows and documentation. | The whole diagram with your marks |
| Mermaid diagram (`.mmd`, `.mermaid`) | Click a node, an edge or a label, then **Add to prompt** (<kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>L</kbd>). The chip holds what it is, its text, a flowchart node's links in and out, and the source line that names it. | The diagram as shown, with your marks |
| Excalidraw drawing | Select shapes, then **Add to prompt** | The view with your marks, or **Add to chat** in the bar for the whole canvas |
| Image | — | **Add to chat** in the bar attaches the picture itself |

## What the agent receives

Chips wait above your text until you send. Click a chip's X to remove it, or hover it to read the start of what it holds. On send, each chip's context goes ahead of your text, in the order you added them. A code selection, for example, arrives as `Selected context — server.js (lines 7-13):` followed by the lines.

<Frame caption="A chip waits above your text until you send.">
  <img src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/suyash-lyzr/alpha-documentation@0bce4e6aa460e3426daece54f29c495f4565999a/images/workspace/pointing-chips-composer.webp" alt="The composer of the Harbor bookings session with one chip above the typed text Make this headline smaller on phones. The chip reads h1 and the quoted start of the headline, Good days start on the wa…, with a small X on its right." />

The transcript shows the same chips above your sent text. If a send fails, the chips come back. Opening another session drops chips you haven't sent, and editing a [queued message](/chat/queue-and-steer) turns its chips into plain text.

<Tip>
Pair a pointer with one short sentence of intent. "Make this match the header" with an element chip beats a paragraph describing where the button is.
</Tip>
